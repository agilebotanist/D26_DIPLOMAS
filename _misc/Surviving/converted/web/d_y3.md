	#### Why does AI boost individual productivity but not organizations?
	AI Summary
	AI is enhancing individual productivity but failing to translate these gains into broader organizational efficiency. This paradox arises because many companies have not adapted their processes to fully leverage AI's capabilities, leading to a disconnect between personal and team productivity.
	##### Understanding the AI Productivity Paradox
	The challenges organizations face with AI implementation are significant.
	- **Individual Gains** — AI tools are reported to speed up work for individuals, with 96% of companies not seeing a return on investment.
	- **Team Dysfunction** — Despite individual productivity boosts, teams often become more dysfunctional, complicating collaboration.
	- **Fragmentation Tax** — Atlassian estimates that this disconnect costs Fortune 500 companies around $161 billion annually.
	- **Process Misalignment** — Many organizations have not adjusted their workflows to accommodate AI, hindering overall productivity.
	- **Successful Integration** — Companies that effectively integrate AI as a connective layer across their organization see better results.
