## Midjourney Founding Story And Team Structure

- Midjourney was founded by David Holz in **August 2021**, per Sacra. Holz previously co-founded Leap Motion in 2011, which Ultrahaptics acquired in May 2019 for approximately **$30 million**.
- The company describes itself as a “self-funded research lab” rather than a traditional startup, a phrase Holz used directly in the August 2022 Register interview.
- The original team prioritized engineers over operators, with no dedicated sales or business development staff in the early years.
- Holz framed the philosophy bluntly in the same interview: “We’re not like a startup that raises a lot of money and then isn’t sure what their product is and loses money for a long time. We’re like a self-funded research lab.”
- Per Sacra, the team operates remotely with a small core in San Francisco, where the company is registered.
- Decision-making sits with engineering and research staff rather than a layered management hierarchy, a pattern Holz attributed to keeping the team under 50 for as long as practical.

The lean structure makes Midjourney a hard comparison case for any AI firm scaling beyond a few hundred staff. SQ Magazine’s coverage of the [Claude vs ChatGPT comparison data](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/claude-vs-chatgpt-statistics/) shows that Anthropic and OpenAI both crossed 1,000 employees, while Midjourney sat under 200.
