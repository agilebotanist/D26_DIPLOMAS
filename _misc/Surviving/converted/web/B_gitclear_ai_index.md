## Canonical List of Data-Backed AI Developer Velocity and Code Quality Research from 2025-2026

GitClear's first-party research has been cited by [numerous reputable sources](/press_mentions), including MIT Technology Review, TechCrunch and The New Stack. But we don't have the monopoly on great, data-backed research. This page collects widely-cited third-party research on AI developer productivity and code quality between 2024 and 2026. If you have a link to AI research that has changed your thinking, [let us know](mailto:hello@gitclear.com)?
