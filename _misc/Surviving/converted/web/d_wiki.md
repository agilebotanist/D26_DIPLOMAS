**Miro** is a [collaborative online platform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collaborative_consumption "Collaborative consumption") designed to support teams in planning and execution across different locations and time zones. It is used for a variety of purposes, including [brainstorming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brainstorming "Brainstorming"), agile planning, customer journey mapping, product design, and remote workshops. As of 2025, the platform is utilized by over 90 million users and more than 250,000 organizations worldwide.[^2]

## History

### Founding and early years (2011–2020)

Miro was founded in 2011 under the name RealtimeBoard by Russian entrepreneurs Andrey Khusid and Oleg Shardin.[^3][^4] Originally designed as a simple visual whiteboarding tool, the product attracted global remote teams because its real-time collaboration features differentiated it from traditional diagramming software.

In 2018, the company raised $25 million in Series A funding led by [Accel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accel_\(company\) "Accel (company)"), with participation from [Altair Capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altair_Capital "Altair Capital") and [Scale Venture Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scale_Venture_Partners "Scale Venture Partners").[^5] By then, it had grown to over 2 million users.

In 2019, the company rebranded to Miro, inspired by the Catalan artist [Joan Miró](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Mir%C3%B3 "Joan Miró"), to reflect its vision beyond whiteboarding into end-to-end visual collaboration.[^6]

### Global expansion and investment (2021–2023)

In January 2022, Miro raised $400 million in Series C funding, co-led by ICONIQ Growth, Accel, [Atlassian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlassian "Atlassian"), [Dragoneer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragoneer_Investment_Group "Dragoneer Investment Group"), GIC, and [Scale Venture Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scale_Venture_Partners "Scale Venture Partners"), reaching a valuation of $17.5 billion.[^7][^8]

The company has expanded its global workforce and is jointly headquartered in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam "Amsterdam") and [San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco "San Francisco").[^9] It has also established offices in [Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas") and [Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney "Sydney").

The [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic") accelerated Miro's adoption, as teams sought cloud-native [remote work](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remote_work "Remote work") alternatives to in-office whiteboards and meeting rooms.[^10]

### Becoming an innovation workspace (2024–present)

In 2024, Miro started its transition from visual collaboration platform to innovation workspace.[^11] The company aims to tackle common organizational challenges that impede innovation, such as fragmented workflows and communication breakdowns.[^12]

In September 2026, the Italian software company [Bending Spoons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bending_Spoons "Bending Spoons") agreed to acquire Miro.[^13]

## CEO and leadership

Andrey Khusid, co-founder and CEO, is an advocate of product-led growth and distributed work models.[^14]

Jeff Chow, chief product and technology officer since 2022, previously worked at [Google](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google "Google") and [Tripadvisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripadvisor "Tripadvisor"), and leads Miro's AI and platform innovation.[^15]

## Acquisitions

In October 2022, Miro acquired video conferencing startup Around to enhance synchronous collaboration directly within the Miro canvas.[^16]

In June 2024, Miro acquired Danish AI design startup Uizard, strengthening its generative interface and rapid prototyping capabilities.[^17]

In 2026, Miro acquired Edtech company, Reforge.[^18]

## Events

### Distributed and Canvas

From 2017 to 2023, Miro hosted an annual virtual conference called **Distributed** focused on remote collaboration and product updates.[^19][^20] In 2024, the event was rebranded as **Canvas** and held in a hybrid format. At Canvas 2024, Miro introduced the Innovation Workspace, which *SD Times* described as "the company's most significant platform update since its founding."[^21]
