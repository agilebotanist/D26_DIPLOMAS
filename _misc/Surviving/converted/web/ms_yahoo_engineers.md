## What Microsoft Has Actually Committed To

Chief People Officer Amy Coleman opened the offer to employees at level 70 and below on participating teams in the U.S., Canada, India, and the U.K., with financial support and career planning help.

Microsoft confirmed that about 60,000 people are eligible, which is roughly a quarter of its 223,000 employees. There is no age or tenure test. Eligible staff will be decided by early December, and anyone who accepts will leave on December 31.
