
***With AI Investment Accelerating Across the Technology Stack, IT Spending Will Cross the $7 Trillion Mark for the First Time***

ORLANDO, Fla.--([BUSINESS WIRE](https://www.businesswire.com/))--Worldwide IT spending is expected to reach $7.35 trillion in 2027, up 15% from 2026, according to the latest forecast by Gartner, Inc., a business and technology insights company.

Gartner analysts are discussing the trends that are impacting the IT market during [Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.gartner.com%2Fen%2Fconferences%2Fna%2Fsymposium-us&esheet=54626296&newsitemid=20261021492736&lan=en-US&anchor=Gartner+IT+Symposium%2FXpo&index=1&md5=2e1b995fb5ba2672b3f878d765670e90), taking place here through Thursday.

“Collectively, the market forces are reinforcing spend around AI infrastructure, AI-enabled cloud platforms and advanced software ecosystems, as these are the most attractive opportunities in global IT spending,” said [John-David Lovelock](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.gartner.com%2Fen%2Fexperts%2Fjohn-lovelock&esheet=54626296&newsitemid=20261021492736&lan=en-US&anchor=John-David+Lovelock&index=2&md5=c797c6bda40afc93233921622660bc0f), Distinguished VP Analyst at Gartner. “Increasingly, the growth of the digital economy is being determined not only by software adoption, but also by the ability to finance, build and monetize AI infrastructure at an unprecedented scale.”

The next $1 trillion dollars in IT spending growth is being shaped by two interconnected [AI](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.gartner.com%2Fen%2Findustries%2Fhigh-tech%2Fwinning-the-ai-vendor-race&esheet=54626296&newsitemid=20261021492736&lan=en-US&anchor=AI&index=3&md5=573240cef92e930e6e8cab6eec45aa3c) domains: AI infrastructure and AI software. Spending on AI-optimized servers, AI-optimized infrastructure as a service (IaaS) and AI software platforms continues to strengthen as organizations seek to industrialize AI deployment. At the same time, hardware markets are experiencing growing pressure from memory-related inflation (see Table 1).

Over the past 12 months, demand from data center systems has driven memory prices to more than double, with cost increases cascading throughout storage, server, networking, PC and device supply chains.

“This ‘memflation’ effect is expected to persist through 2027 before hardware pricing begins to stabilize,” said Lovelock. “While higher average selling prices continue to support spending growth, the resulting pressure on affordability is expected to suppress end-user demand throughout the forecast horizon.”

Longer term, the forecast reflects a further upward revision in growth expectations for data center systems, reinforcing the view that AI infrastructure has become the foundational layer of the AI economy. Unlike previous technology cycles where infrastructure supported software growth, infrastructure itself is increasingly being treated as a strategic investment asset.

“Backed by hyperscaler capital expenditure, private capital funding, financing initiatives and in some cases the technology suppliers themselves, AI infrastructure is emerging as both a technology platform and an investable asset class,” said Lovelock. “As a result, spending on AI infrastructure, AI [cloud](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.gartner.com%2Fen%2Finfrastructure-and-it-operations-leaders%2Ftopics%2Fcloud-strategy&esheet=54626296&newsitemid=20261021492736&lan=en-US&anchor=cloud&index=4&md5=90881b735c55d405411ca77bba7bf1b3) services, AI platforms and AI-enabled software is expected to reach explosive highs throughout the forecast period, underpinning sustained growth in the broader IT market.”

Gartner’s IT spending forecast methodology relies heavily on rigorous analysis of the sales by over a thousand vendors across the entire range of IT products and services. Gartner uses primary research techniques, complemented by secondary research sources, to build a comprehensive database of market size data on which to base its forecast.
