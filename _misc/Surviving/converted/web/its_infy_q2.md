# Infosys hires 4,480 employees in Q2FY27

**URL:** https://www.deccanherald.com/business/companies/infosys-hires-4480-employees-in-q2fy27-4193101
**Description:** Infosys Q2 results: IT giant adds 4480 net employees and plans to hire over 20000 freshers this fiscal year, signaling a steady recovery in tech sector jobs as major Indian firms report workforce growth during the second quarter.
**Scraped:** 2026-11-17T15:05:00.744800+00:00

---

\<p>Bengaluru: In a sign of revival for IT jobs, Infosys added 4,480 employees sequentially in Q2, bucking the trend of headcount cuts and hiring freezes. The company's total headcount stood at 3,32,542 as of September 30, 2026. However, on a YoY basis, it added only 551 employees.\<br>\<br>Salil Parekh, CEO and MD of Infosys said that they also hired 8,000 freshers in the second quarter. "For the full year, target remains at about 20,000. We have already done more than half in the first half of the year," he said.\</p>.Infosys reduces FY27 revenue growth guidance to 1-2%.\<p>Jayesh Sanghrajka, CFO of Infosys, said that they hire across the pyramid. "Even we are hiring at the bottom of the pyramid, which is the freshers, and we are going to hire 20,000 plus employees over the year. We are very conscious about hiring the right talent to meet the right requirement," he added.\</p>\<p>Commenting on Infosys' hiring,  Praveen Bhadada, CEO & Managing Director, Neovay Global, said Infosys’s headcount is flat from a year ago, the other large Indian firms grew theirs by less than 1%, and Accenture grew by 5%.\<br>\<br>"Firms are relying on AI and productivity to deliver new work rather than hiring ahead of demand," he said.\<br>\<br>Infosys' peers HCLTech and TCS too increased headcount by 6,450 and 4,258 employees, respectively. However, Wipro's headcount declined by 6,716 employees in the second quarter.\</p>
