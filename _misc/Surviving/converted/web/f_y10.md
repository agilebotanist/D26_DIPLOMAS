	#### What are per-seat AI pricing models?
	AI Summary
	Per-seat AI add-on pricing models charge customers based on the number of users or seats that access the AI service. This model is popular in SaaS (Software as a Service) environments, allowing businesses to scale their usage according to their needs while providing predictable costs for budgeting purposes.
	##### Overview of Per-Seat AI Pricing Models
	Understanding the key aspects of per-seat pricing can help businesses make informed decisions.
	- **Cost Structure** — Pricing is typically calculated as a fixed fee per user per month or year, making it easy to predict expenses.
	- **Scalability** — Businesses can easily add or remove seats as their needs change, allowing for flexible scaling of AI capabilities.
	- **User Management** — This model often includes features for managing user access and permissions, enhancing security and control over the AI tools.
	- **Value Proposition** — Companies may find this model beneficial as it aligns costs directly with usage, ensuring they only pay for what they need.
