### Gartner Predicts That By 2030, Agency Fees Will Represent 15% of Marketing Budgets, Down From 24.5% At The Start of the Decade

Marketing

As AI transforms marketing, it is also reshaping how brands work with agencies, from agency roster models to pricing. As marketers automate more work, build capabilities in-house and use specialized partners more selectively, Gartner predicts agency fees will decline from 24.5% of marketing budgets at the start of the decade to 15% by 2030.

These changes will require CMOs to rethink how they engage agencies and how they compensate them. We spoke with [Jay Wilson](https://www.gartner.com/en/analysts/jay-wilson), VP Analyst in the [Gartner Marketing practice](https://www.gartner.com/en/marketing), about how AI is changing agency economics, staffing models and the future of client-agency partnerships.
