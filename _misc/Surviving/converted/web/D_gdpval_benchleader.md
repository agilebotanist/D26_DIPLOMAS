## What the test looks like

GDPval asks models to produce real work products, such as a legal memo, a financial model or a design brief, across 44 occupations that carry the most economic weight. Industry professionals then compare each output against a human expert's.

## How it is scored

Win rate: how often graders preferred (or tied) the model's deliverable against the professional's. Published by OpenAI.

## What to keep in mind

Grading is human and pairwise, so the score is relative and expensive to reproduce; only a gold subset is public. The tasks are one-shot deliverables, not the iterative work most jobs involve.

## GDPval: questions

What does GDPval measure?

GDPval asks models to produce real work products, such as a legal memo, a financial model or a design brief, across 44 occupations that carry the most economic weight. Industry professionals then compare each output against a human expert's. Scores are reported in percent of tasks solved; higher is better.

What is the top score on GDPval?

As of 17 Nov 2026, the highest score on GDPval is 49.7% by GPT-5.2 (result dated 11 Dec 2025), ahead of Claude Opus 4.5 at 45.5%. Scores come from OpenAI and are re-read daily.

Which AI model leads GDPval?

GPT-5.2 leads GDPval with 49.7% as of 17 Nov 2026 (result dated 11 Dec 2025), ahead of Claude Opus 4.5 at 45.5%.

How many models have GDPval results?

11 model configurations have a GDPval result on BenchLeader, all taken from OpenAI via Epoch AI Benchmarking Hub.

Who runs GDPval and how often is it updated?

GDPval is published by OpenAI. BenchLeader re-reads the published results once a day and records the date each result was published.

Does GDPval count toward the BenchLeader Index?

Yes. GDPval contributes to the agents & tools category of the BenchLeader Index, normalised so that 50 is the average of the evaluated models.
