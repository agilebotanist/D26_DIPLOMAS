[Satya Nadella](https://www.businessinsider.com/satya-nadella-ai-alignment-changed-mind-different-problem-scales-2026-10) has tips for software engineers who are anxious about AI.

Speaking on an episode of "The Pragmatic Engineer" podcast released on Monday, the Microsoft CEO said that one of the best ways for early-career software engineers to learn in the AI era is to get on a team building with the technology.

"It's just not about the hype cycle, and that constant need to talk," Nadella told podcast host Gergely Orosz. "But it's do more, learn more, and hands-on experience — experience it firsthand."

"If one piece of advice, whether you're mid-career or even a CEO like me or a fresh college grad, the more you can take the time to sort of learn to do, be in teams that are doing, the better it is versus, I think, anything else," Nadella added.

Nadella's advice comes as software engineering hiring shows signs of rebounding, but opportunities remain uneven. Nearly 69.3% of US [software development job](https://www.businessinsider.com/software-engineer-jobs-rebounding-senior-tech-roles-hiring-job-market-2026-8) postings in the first quarter of 2026 were for senior-level positions, up from 54.5% in early 2019, according to Indeed data.

Nadella said that companies need to create more opportunities for junior workers to learn these new ways of building software.

"I care a lot about, hey, can you have an early-career person come in, join one of these teams, go up the learning curve," Nadella said. "So what's the new form of that is what I think we have to really discover."

Nadella said Microsoft recently launched PRAISE, or Preceptorship for AI-accelerated Software Engineering, with Carnegie Mellon University. The program pairs early-career engineers with senior mentors to build AI-assisted development skills.

"In a world where code generation has become cheap, what has become hard is verification," Nadella said. "There's just so much, I would say, new type of [tribal knowledge](https://www.businessinsider.com/goldman-sachs-engineers-ai-marco-argenti-2026-8) people are building up in managing complex software projects."

"There's an entire new way to build that is emerging, and it's new software engineering," Nadella added, "And it requires software engineers."
