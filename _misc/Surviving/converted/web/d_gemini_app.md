## Where you can use the Gemini web app

The Gemini web app is currently available in more than 70 languages and over 230 countries and territories.

We’ll gradually expand to more countries and territories in a way that's consistent with local regulations and our [AI principles](https://ai.google/principles/).

**Important:**

- The Gemini mobile app may have different availability. Learn more about the [supported languages and countries of the mobile app](/gemini/answer/14579026).
- Learn more about eligibility and requirements to use [the Gemini web app](/gemini/answer/13278668) or [the Gemini mobile app](/gemini/answer/14554984).
