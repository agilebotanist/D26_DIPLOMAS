## Overview

We provide below a list of the countries and territories in which we currently support access to ChatGPT on web and mobile, and to the OpenAI API.

Accessing or offering access to our services outside of the countries and territories listed below may result in your account being blocked or suspended.

OpenAI does not publish a separate list of countries and territories that are not supported. If a location is not included in the list below, our API is not available there.

## If you receive the error "OpenAI's services are not available in your country."

You may see an unsupported-country error when trying to sign up or create an API key from a location we don’t support. Check the list below to confirm whether the country or territory you’re accessing our services from is included.

You can also encounter this error in a supported location—for example, after using your device in an unsupported region and returning home. These browser troubleshooting steps apply only if you’re currently in a supported country or territory:

1. Clear your browser’s cache and cookies.
2. Try a different browser or an incognito window.

For more information, see our [guidance on access while traveling and unsupported-country errors](https://help.openai.com/articles/9131992).
