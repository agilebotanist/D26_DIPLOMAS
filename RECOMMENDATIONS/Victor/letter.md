To the Admissions Committee,

I am writing in support of Viktor Vanichkov's application to your PhD program. I am an Associate Professor at Innopolis University and a Research Project Manager at SOFTEAM, part of Docaposte (Groupe La Poste). Viktor was my teaching assistant at Innopolis University over several years, in the Master's courses Managing Software Development and Analysis of Software Artifacts (Quality Assurance Management) during the academic years 2023-2024, 2024-2025 and 2025-2026. My comments are based on that work, and I cannot speak to his research in reinforcement learning, which I have not supervised.

Both courses are demanding. Homework can reach up to 18 hours per week and is varied: case analyses, reading questions, quality plans and presentations, alongside regular sessions with students. Grading this material has to be thorough and finished on time if students are to learn from it. Viktor took on this load repeatedly and returned his feedback on schedule, semester after semester. As a supervisor, I could rely on him to meet deadlines without reminders.

He worked well with students. He consulted them on coursework and homework and handled their questions in a way that kept them engaged with the material His feedback went beyond marking answers right or wrong. On the critical reasoning questions he pushed students to separate facts from assumptions and to trace problems back to root causes. On the quality plans he assessed whether the plan could actually be carried out in practice, which is a harder and more useful judgment than checking that every required section is present.

What I can say with confidence is that Viktor is dependable, communicates well with students, and carries sustained academic work to completion. Those habits matter in a doctoral program, where progress depends on steady work over years.
He is returning to research after substantial industry experience in software architecture and technical leadership, and he has described to me his current work on heterogeneous federated reinforcement learning. I leave the assessment of that work to those who have reviewed it directly.

I am glad to support his application and can be reached for any questions.
