## Method

Rubric (100 pts, written work): A problem, goal, RQ (25); B literature (35); C method and plan (25); D structure and writing (15). Each thesis was read in full by an independent reviewer, who also spot-checked 3 references per thesis against the web. Texts: the latest PDFs in `~/Downloads` (2026-11-01 versions).

BSc students of the cohort: raw = 0.85 x written + 0.15 x oral, where oral = mean of the three preliminary colloquium marks (theme, literature, experiment, 1-5) x 20, from `<Student>/feedback/colloquium-*.md`.

SNE students (supervised with Pierre-Philipp Braun): no colloquium marks exist, so raw = mean of my independent written score and Pierre's grade (x10). Both are shown so the blend can be overridden.

Letter thresholds (supervisor, 2026-11-06): A 90.00-100, B 80.00-89.99, C 70.00-79.99. The table has no row below C; I read anything under 70 as not reaching C and label it "below C". Confirm what that band should be called. Stage adjustment: the reviewers scored on the full scale, where 90+ means a near-final text, which no chapters 1-2 draft can reach, so raw scores sit low for this stage. At the supervisor's request (most students should land in A, B or C, below C only for very poor work) a flat +9 is added to the raw composite, capped at 100. Chapters 3 and later are out of scope: the reviewers' part D deductions for unfinished later chapters and template leftovers were reversed (+2 written for Elizaveta and Aleksandr, who had them). The abstract is also a final-stage item, so the part of part D that reviewers took off for a missing or placeholder abstract was reversed (+1 written for Aziz, Darya, Danil, Ilya, Elizaveta, Aleksandr and Arsen, whose reviews cited it). The shift keeps the ranking and the gaps between students unchanged; the raw composite stays visible in the table. After the shift only Arsen stays below C.

Rubric alignment with the university documents received 2026-11-06 (stored in `work/regulations/`): Thesis Guidelines 2022 §3.4 (introduction: problem, importance, known and unknown, hypotheses or solutions, research questions, structure outline) and §3.5 (literature review: relevant prior work, classification and evaluation of methods and metrics, justification of the chosen method), and §4.3 assessment criteria (explicit hypothesis or RQ, validation criteria, measurable outcomes, representative and properly cited reading, own words). Regulations 6.9: English or Russian, title page, contents, introduction (relevance, goal, tasks, novelty), literature review. These items are covered by rubric parts A-D, so the written scores stand. The abstract is a final-stage item and is not assessed at this stage, nor are chapters 3 and later.

Master's students (Ilnar, Yuri) are out of scope and were not graded.

Контейнер может жить на любом узле, в одном экземпляре или в нескольких репликах, хранилище строится на тех же узлах (конвергенция), поэтому нужен раздел про локальные, сетевые и конвергентные хранилища, PV и CSI и последствия перепланирования, и явный выбор допущения; (2) определиться с направлением, как предложил консультант: диск плюс пересмотренная архитектура хранения; либо Nomad и проверка на разнородные ресурсы; либо собственный простой планировщик по существующим метрикам без диска; (3) в §1.1 зафиксировать оркестратор, генератор нагрузки (fio, YCSB), метрики (IOPS, p99, дисперсия загрузки узлов), базовые варианты, число повторов, критерий завершённости; (4) довести литературу до 20 рецензируемых источников (cgroup v2 io.max, PSI, scheduler framework, storage-aware scheduling, noisy neighbour), уточнить, чего не хватает в [7], чтобы работа не была повтором; (5) сократить §2.1–2.2 (Raft и устройство планировщика), добавить таблицу сравнения [5], [7], [12], [13]; (6) исправить дату на титульном листе.
